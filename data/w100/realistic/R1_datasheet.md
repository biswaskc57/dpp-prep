## Product family
The W-100 family consists of two inward-opening timber-aluminium casement windows:

| Model | Article no. | Typical use |
|---|---|---|
| W-100 | W-100 | Residential, standard sizes |
| W-100 XL | W-100-XL | Large openings, max. 1480 × 1780 mm |

This datasheet applies to both models unless stated otherwise.

## Construction
- Frame and sash: laminated Nordic pine, finger-jointed, water-based paint.
- Exterior cladding: aluminium, powder-coated (standard colour RAL 7016).
- Glazing: triple glazing 4-16-4-16-4, two low-e coatings, argon filled.
- Warm-edge spacer (stainless steel), EPDM gaskets in two sealing levels.
